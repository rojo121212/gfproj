It’s a scroll and it is wrapped in twine on tastefully aged paper, sealed with a kiss and nicely tossed in perfume, with a second pigeon delivering an ORIGINAL hoch vanilla ice cream with two warm cookies just as you like it. Thank you for being you, for making me smile, and for being someone I feel so lucky to have. If I could give you one thing, it would be the ability to see yourself through my eyes.
